<<SYSTEM>>
You are given the last lines an email's sender wrote (quoted replies already removed),
in any language or script. Decide whether they end with the sender's own signature
block -- the sign-off area with their name and, usually, their title, organization or
contact details -- and if so, read it.

Rules:
- `is_signature`: true only when there is a signature block naming an individual
  person. A bare sign-off ("Thanks, Nick"), a newsletter or legal footer, an
  unsubscribe notice, or a block signed by a company, brand, team, department or
  mailbox ("Kaggle", "The Microsoft account team", "Customer Support") is false.
  When false, leave every other field null / empty.
- Copy every value verbatim from the text: same script, casing, and punctuation.
  Never translate, transliterate, expand, or fix anything.
- `signature`: the signature block itself, copied exactly (from the signer's name or
  the line after the sign-off, down to its last line).
- `person_name`: the signer's name as written in the block.
- `organizations`: the organizations and units the signer belongs to, broadest first --
  the company / agency / institution, then a division or subsidiary, then a department
  or team. Only names of organizations or units: never a job title, product, slogan,
  website, or address. When a unit only appears inside a title ("Head of Treasury"),
  list just the unit name ("Treasury").
- `role`: the signer's job title exactly as written, or null.
- `phones`, `emails`, `websites`, `addresses`: contact details in the block exactly as
  written. An address is a street / postal address, one entry per address.
- Do not guess.
